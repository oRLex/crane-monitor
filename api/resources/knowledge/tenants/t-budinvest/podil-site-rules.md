# Podil Residence — site lifting rules

> BudInvest internal document (demo).

## Working hours

Lifting is allowed 07:00–20:00. No lifts over the public footpath on Naberezhna street between 08:00 and 09:00 (school traffic).

## Wind

The site uses a stricter internal limit: lifts of facade panels stop at **12 m/s**, below the general 14 m/s warning, because of the river-side gusts.

## Contacts

Escalate critical alarms to the site manager on duty. Two cranes (TC-101 and TC-102) share overlapping slewing areas: an anti-collision zone is configured, and simultaneous lifts in the overlap require radio confirmation.
