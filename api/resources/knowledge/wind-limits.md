# Wind limits and out-of-service procedure

> Demo library document. Values match the CraneWatch alarm rules; always follow the manufacturer's manual and local regulations for real equipment.

## Measured wind and alarm thresholds

Wind speed is measured by the anemometer at the top of the crane (jib or A-frame) and reported as a 3-second gust value in m/s.

- **14 m/s — wind warning.** The operator is warned. Lifts with large sail area (formwork panels, cladding, sheet material) must not be started.
- **20 m/s — operating limit (critical).** Stop all lifting operations. Land the load in the shortest safe way, do not leave a load suspended.
- Above **20 m/s** the crane must be put **out of service** (see below).

The warning threshold is lower than the stop threshold on purpose: a load that is already in the air needs time to be landed before gusts reach the operating limit.

## Out-of-service (weathervaning)

1. Land the load and raise the empty hook to the top position, close to the trolley.
2. Move the trolley to the parking radius stated in the crane manual (usually close to the mast).
3. Release the slewing brake so the jib can turn freely with the wind ("weathervaning"). A crane with a locked slew brake in storm wind is exposed to dangerous side loads.
4. Switch the crane off at the main isolator and record the stop time in the logbook.

## Restarting after high wind

- Wait until the 10-minute average is below 14 m/s and gusts stay below the warning threshold.
- Perform a visual check of the jib, counter-jib and ties, and a functional test of limit switches without load.
- Record the restart and the reason in the logbook. Repeated wind stops on a site should be reviewed with the site manager — they often mean lifts need to be planned for the morning hours.

## Frequent wind alarm flapping

If the wind alarm is raised and cleared many times in a short period, the wind is hovering around a threshold. Treat the higher severity as current: keep the crane stopped until conditions have been stable below the warning value for at least 10 minutes. Do not acknowledge alarms just to silence them.
